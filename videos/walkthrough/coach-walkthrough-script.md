# Brophy Soccer: Coach Walkthrough (narration script)

Welcome to Brophy Soccer. This is a quick tour of the app as a coach, so you know where everything lives.

When you sign in, you land on Home. This is your Today page. At the top you see today's schedule, what is happening and when. Below that is team wellness at a glance. You can see who is Ready, who is Modified, and who needs Rest, all in one place. Then come announcements, the quote of the day, your roster, and your documents.

Next is the Coach Center. Tap Coach and you get six tiles. Practice is where you build sessions on the to-scale diagram board. Game Day is where you set lineups. Roster and Invites is where you add coaches and players and make invite links. Then Calendar, Documents, and Announcements.

The Schedule tab shows every team event and lineup in one place. The app sends automatic reminders one hour before each event and again at 7 AM on the day.

Train is where your training content lives. Gym has in-season prehab and band work. Nutrition has recipes and a food search. Videos and Drills give you the drill library and your practice sessions.

Chat keeps everything inside your team. There is the Team channel, group chats, and direct messages with your players. You can run polls, pin messages, and schedule messages ahead of time.

Finally, Settings. People and Roles is where the org admin promotes or demotes coaches and assigns them to teams. And if you coach more than one team, the switcher at the top lets you jump between JVB, JVA, and Varsity.

That is the whole app. Five tabs, everything in one place.
