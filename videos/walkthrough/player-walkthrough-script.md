# Brophy Soccer: Player Walkthrough (narration script)

Welcome to Brophy Soccer. This is a quick tour of the app as a player, so you know where everything lives.

When you sign in, you land on Today. At the top you see today's schedule, what is happening and when. Then comes your wellness check-in. You rate your energy, your soreness, and your tiredness, each from 1 to 5. One means you are wiped out. Five means you are feeling great. Your coach sees the result and knows how you are doing before practice starts. Below that are announcements from your coach, the quote of the day, and your documents.

Train is where your training content lives. Gym has your prehab routines, the band work and activation you do before practice and on match days. Nutrition is where you set your own goal, find recipes, and log your meals and water. Videos has coaching clips to watch. Drills is the drill library, so you can look up anything your coach runs in practice.

The Schedule tab shows your practices, your games, and your lineups, all in one place. You will get a reminder one hour before each event and again at 7 AM on the day, so you never miss one.

Chat keeps everything inside your own team. There is the Team channel for the whole squad, a direct message with your own team's coach, group chats, and polls. You only ever see your own team, and nobody from another team can see your messages.

That is the whole app. The bottom tabs are always there: Today, Train, Schedule, and Chat. And if you open it on a laptop, those same options live in the sidebar on the left. See you at practice.
